### Featured Articles — AI-Driven GRC Features Shipped This Year

- **[Grounded Review Summaries](/articles/2026-grounded-review-summaries/)** — every sub-review summary is generated from retrieved evidence with citations on every claim. Hybrid retrieval (vector + BM25 + entity graph) feeds a Verifier agent that re-checks each citation actually entails the claim before a human ever sees it.
- **[DPIA & LIA Reviews](/articles/2026-dpia-lia-reviews/)** — Data Protection Impact Assessments (GDPR Art. 35) and Legitimate Interest Assessments (Art. 6(1)(f)) authored as structured templates, pre-filled from intake and evidence with citations, and validated by counsel. Cycle time moves from weeks to days while staying regulator-ready.
