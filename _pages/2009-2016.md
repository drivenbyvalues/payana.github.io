## Senior Engineering Manager
**Intuit**  
*Woodland Hills, CA | 2009 - 2016*

<div class="highlight-box">
    <h3>Key Achievements</h3>
    <ul>
        <li>Architected and led development of REST-based microservices processing over $1B+ in annual payments across web and mobile platforms</li>
        <li>Spearheaded Agile transformation for 5+ teams, implementing CI/CD pipelines that reduced deployment time from hours to minutes (75% improvement)</li>
        <li>Successfully delivered critical projects including Activation Platform Services and QuickBooks Point of Sale, serving millions of small businesses</li>
        <li>Led migration from monolithic architecture to microservices, improving system scalability to handle 10x traffic growth</li>
        <li>Established engineering best practices and coding standards adopted by 50+ engineers across multiple teams</li>
    </ul>
</div>

## Technical Leadership
- Led and mentored multiple cross-functional engineering teams (5-8 direct reports, 20+ indirect) in developing scalable payment solutions
- Championed test-driven development, achieving 90%+ test coverage across critical services
- Drove technical innovation through regular proof-of-concepts and technology evaluations
- Established and enforced coding standards, code review processes, and architectural guidelines
- Built high-performance engineering teams through strategic hiring and talent development

## Projects & Impact

        <li>Implemented end-to-end security measures including PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance and tokenization of sensitive data</li>
        <li>Integrated with 15+ payment gateways and financial institutions, standardizing interfaces and reducing integration time by 60%</li>
